Privacy Policy - Vaadivasal (வாடிவாசல்)

Effective date: 6 October 2026
App: Vaadivasal - Android app (com.dineshrichard.vaadivasal)
Developer: Dinesh Richard

Summary

Vaadivasal does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no connection to the internet.

What the app does and does not do

  • No data collection. The app does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or any other personal data.
  • No permissions. The app requests no Android permissions. In particular it does not have the internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your device.
  • No ads, trackers or third-party services. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social SDKs, and does not use any third-party online service.
  • No accounts, no purchases. There is nothing to sign in to and nothing to buy inside the app.
  • Local files only. On first launch the app copies the game file (the Vaadivasal ROM, which is bundled inside the app) into its own private storage so the game can run. Nothing about you or how you play is written there, and no other app can read it. Game progress is kept only in memory while the app is running.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13, because it collects no information from anyone.

Deleting your data

There is no personal data to delete. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your device.

Open source

The app is open source (GPL v2; the game itself is MIT). You can read exactly what it does at github.com/DineshRichard/Vaadivasal-NES.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated and the effective date above changed before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy: please open an issue at github.com/DineshRichard/Vaadivasal-NES/issues.
