## Full description (max 4000)
Vaadivasal (வாடிவாசல்) is the first 8-bit game made entirely in Tamil - a retro tribute to the ancient sport of Jallikattu.

Step into the Alanganallur arena and face 25 legendary bulls. Dodge the charging bull, wait for it to tire, then catch it with lightning-fast button presses. Tame the bull. Honor the tradition.

HOW TO PLAY
1. Dodge - move with the D-pad and stay clear of the charging bull.
2. Wait - when the bull stops and blinks, it is tired.
3. Grab - run up and touch the bull.
4. The Lock - five buttons flash on screen. Press each one quickly (A, B or a direction) before the time runs out.
Catch all 25 bulls to win. You have three lives.

FEATURES
• Made in Tamil - the game and its screens are in Tamil
• 25 bulls, each faster than the last
• Dodging plus quick-reaction button challenges that get tighter as you progress
• Authentic 8-bit pixel art and a chiptune soundtrack, all made by one developer
• Comfortable touch controls (D-pad, A, B, Start, Select); gamepads work too
• Pause any time with Start
• No ads. No in-app purchases. No internet needed. No permissions
• Free and open source

The game runs on the FCEUmm NES emulator core (GNU GPL v2). Source code: https://github.com/DineshRichard/Vaadivasal-NES

Vaadivasal is an independent homebrew project and is not affiliated with Nintendo.
